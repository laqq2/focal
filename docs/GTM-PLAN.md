# Focal Go-To-Market Plan (90 Days)

## Strategic Focus
- Beachhead audience: students/researchers and independent knowledge workers.
- Primary channel mix: build-in-public content + communities + Product Hunt + Chrome Web Store listing optimization.
- Core message: "One tab for calm, intentional focus."

## Phase 1: Foundation (Weeks 1-3)
### Goals
- Instrument funnel and baseline metrics.
- Strengthen onboarding and value communication.
- Recruit beta feedback cohort.

### Actions
- Implement analytics events from `docs/ANALYTICS-SPEC.md`.
- Add onboarding checklist in app.
- Recruit 20-30 early users (personal network, study/productivity communities).
- Replace representative testimonials with real quotes + consent.

## Phase 2: Proof and Conversion (Weeks 4-8)
### Goals
- Improve retention and trial starts.
- Establish social proof and narrative consistency.

### Actions
- Publish weekly product updates with a single metric trend.
- Run 3 pricing/paywall experiments:
  - monthly vs annual emphasis
  - paywall trigger timing
  - trial length (7 vs 14 days)
- Launch lifecycle messaging:
  - onboarding day 0/day 2/day 7 emails
  - weekly review prompt
  - trial conversion sequence

## Phase 3: Launch and Scale (Weeks 9-12)
### Goals
- Execute Product Hunt launch.
- Capture paying users and preserve post-launch momentum.

### Actions
- Publish Product Hunt listing with full asset pack.
- Coordinate launch day outreach (maker comment, communities, email list, social posts).
- Publish post-launch roadmap and "what's next" update within 72 hours.

## Distribution Channels
- Product Hunt
- Chrome Web Store (listing polish, reviews, frequent changelog updates)
- X/LinkedIn build-in-public
- Niche communities: Reddit productivity/deep work/students
- Micro creators in productivity and ADHD niches

## Messaging Pillars
- Calm > hustle.
- Reflection > streak obsession.
- Browser-native flow where distraction actually happens.

## Weekly Operating Cadence
- Monday: metric review + experiment decision.
- Wednesday: ship day (feature or iteration).
- Friday: narrative day (changelog + public update + user outreach).
- Sunday: internal retention review.

## KPIs
- Topline: MRR, paid subscribers, net revenue retention.
- Funnel: signup -> first session -> day 7 active -> trial -> paid.
- Product: sessions per active user/week, weekly review completion.

## Owners (single-founder default)
- Product + engineering: founder
- Growth + content: founder
- Support: founder
- Optional support contractor once >100 paying users
