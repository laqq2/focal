# Product Hunt Launch Playbook

## Should Focal Launch on Product Hunt?
Yes, after launch readiness checks pass:
- >= 20 active users in the last 14 days
- Real testimonials available
- Billing and trial flows tested
- Clear onboarding and value proof in-app

## Timeline
### T-14 to T-7 days
- Finalize tagline and first comment draft.
- Record 45-60 second demo video.
- Prepare 6 screenshots and maker story.
- Build outreach list (friends, users, communities).

### T-6 to T-1 days
- Soft review listing copy.
- QA signup, onboarding, trial, billing.
- Prepare launch-day posting schedule.

### Launch Day
- Post listing early morning target timezone.
- Publish maker comment immediately.
- Share launch post on X/LinkedIn/email/community channels.
- Respond to every comment within 30 minutes during peak window.

### T+1 to T+7
- Thank supporters.
- Share results transparently.
- Convert traffic with onboarding nudges and trial offer.

## Listing Assets
- Name: Focal
- Tagline (candidate): "A calm new tab for deep work and weekly reflection."
- Description:
  "Focal turns your browser into a calm focus workspace with intentional session starts, distraction blocking, and reflection loops that improve your week."
- Gallery:
  1) Hero: timer + intention
  2) Blocker flow
  3) Learn/weekly reflection
  4) Calendar + daily priorities
  5) Settings/privacy trust card
  6) Pricing/Pro value

## Maker Comment Template
Hello Product Hunt. I built Focal because most productivity tools made me feel more anxious, not more focused.

Focal is designed around a simple loop:
1) set an intention
2) focus in a protected session
3) close the loop with reflection

I would love feedback on three things:
- what made your first session easy or hard
- what you wish the blocker did better
- what would make Pro genuinely worth paying for

Thanks for trying it.

## Launch Day Checklist
- Listing live
- Maker comment posted
- Website/API healthy
- Support inbox monitored
- In-app "welcome Product Hunt" message live
- End-of-day retrospective written
